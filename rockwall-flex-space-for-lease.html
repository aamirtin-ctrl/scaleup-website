Why this site, specifically

Fig. 1 — N Goliad frontage, looking south

Highway frontage on N Goliad

Five acres fronting Goliad Street, which is the north-south spine through Rockwall. That means your signage is seen by daily traffic, and your customers can find you without a map.

Two of the buildings put showroom glass directly toward the road — the units where visibility does real work for revenue.

Fig. 2 — Five-acre parcel, ETJ boundary

ETJ parcel: no city zoning

The site sits in Rockwall's extraterritorial jurisdiction, outside city limits. City zoning does not apply, which widens the range of permitted tenant uses considerably — and is why a contractor, a showroom business, and a fitness studio can share one park.

We wrote the full explanation in what ETJ status means for a flex lease, including what it does not exempt you from.

Who the park is close to

Goliad Street is SH-205, which runs north to south through Rockwall and meets I-30 a few minutes below the site. That puts the park inside a normal working drive of a wide stretch of the eastern side of the metro, so tenants do not have to live in Rockwall to run a business out of it.

The operators we talk to most are based in Rockwall itself, Royse City and Fate just east on I-30, Heath and McLendon-Chisholm to the south, Rowlett and Garland to the west, and Forney and Terrell to the southeast. Wylie and Greenville are further out and still routine for crews already running jobs across the corridor.

If your crew starts the day at the shop and scatters to job sites, the highway you get on matters more than the city on your mail. Ask us for a drive time from wherever your people actually live before you rule the site in or out.

The park delivers Summer 2027, and units are being reserved now. Early tenants choose their building, their end cap, and their door orientation. Later tenants take what is left.

Who leases here

The tenant mix in a small-bay park is wider than most people expect. At this size and spec, these are the businesses that fit best.

  • 01 Contractors & trades HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and roofing crews that need to stage jobs, store material, and park a truck inside overnight.
  • 02 E-commerce & wholesale Inventory space plus a real desk and a shipping door — replacing a garage and a storage unit.
  • 03 Showroom businesses Flooring, outdoor kitchens, golf carts, furniture, building materials — anywhere customers need to see the product.
  • 04 Makers & fabricators Cabinet shops, sign shops, and light assembly that need 3-phase power and a tall clear bay.
  • 05 Service businesses Appliance repair, landscaping, pest control, and mobile services that run crews out of a central base.

Rates and lease structure

Units at Rockwall Flex Park lease on a triple-net (NNN) basis, the standard structure for flex and industrial space in North Texas. You pay a base rate per square foot per year, plus your proportional share of property taxes, building insurance, and common-area maintenance.

Current rates follow the leasing rate plan and move with availability, so we quote them directly rather than posting a number that goes stale.

Common questions

What size flex spaces are available in Rockwall?

Thirty units in three sizes: 1,500 SF standard bays, 2,000 SF large bays, and 3,000 SF showroom units with glass storefronts. Adjacent units can often be combined for larger requirements.

When will Rockwall Flex Park be ready to occupy?

Delivery is scheduled for Summer 2027. The site broke ground in March 2026 and is currently in site work and foundations. Pre-leasing is open now, and early tenants get first choice of units and end caps.

What is included in each flex unit?

A 12×12 grade-level roll-up door, 18 foot clear height, individual HVAC, 3-phase power, LED lighting, a finished office or showroom front, a restroom, and parking directly at your door.

Where exactly is Rockwall Flex Park located?

4156 N Goliad Street in Rockwall, Texas, on five acres with frontage on Goliad. It sits on an ETJ parcel, which means no city zoning applies and a wider range of tenant uses is permitted.

How much does flex space cost in Rockwall?

Small-bay flex in this submarket generally quotes in the mid-teens per square foot per year on a triple-net basis, plus NNN charges. Contact us for the current rate sheet for your unit size.
