Why this site, specifically

Rockwall sits on the eastern edge of the DFW growth path, and the small-bay inventory here has not kept up with the businesses moving in. Our own market research put Rockwall flex around $16.30 per square foot against roughly $9.78 for DFW industrial overall, with flex vacancy near 4.5% versus 9.4% market-wide. Tight supply is why units in this submarket lease before they finish.

Highway frontage on N Goliad

Five acres fronting Goliad Street, which is the north-south spine through Rockwall. That means your signage is seen by daily traffic, and your customers can find you without a map. Two of the buildings put showroom glass directly toward the road.

ETJ parcel: no city zoning

The site sits in Rockwall's extraterritorial jurisdiction, outside city limits. City zoning does not apply, which widens the range of permitted tenant uses considerably and is why a contractor, a showroom business, and a fitness studio can share one park. We wrote the full explanation in what ETJ status means for a flex lease — including what it does not exempt you from.

Pre-leasing advantage: the park delivers Summer 2027, and units are being reserved now. Early tenants choose their building, their end cap, and their door orientation. Later tenants take what is left.

Who leases here

The tenant mix in a small-bay park is wider than most people expect. At this size and spec, the businesses that fit best are:

  • Contractors and trades — HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing crews that need to stage jobs, store material, and park a truck inside overnight.
  • E-commerce and wholesale — inventory space plus a real desk and a shipping door, replacing a garage and a storage unit.
  • Showroom businesses — flooring, outdoor kitchens, golf carts, furniture, building materials, where customers need to see the product.
  • Makers and fabricators — cabinet shops, sign shops, and light assembly that need 3-phase power and a tall clear bay.
  • Service businesses — appliance repair, landscaping, pest control, and mobile services that run crews out of a central base.

Rates and lease structure

Units at Rockwall Flex Park lease on a triple-net (NNN) basis, the standard structure for flex and industrial space in North Texas. You pay a base rate per square foot per year, plus your proportional share of property taxes, building insurance, and common-area maintenance.
