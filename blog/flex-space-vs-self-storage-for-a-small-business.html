Flex space vs self-storage: which does your small business need?

By the ScaleUp principals — 90+ combined years in construction, industrial operations & finance Published August 7, 2026 6 min read

Self-storage is priced to hold things and a flex bay is priced to work in, so the deciding question is whether anyone spends the day in the space. If nothing happens in the unit except boxes going in and coming out, storage does the job. The moment a person, a tool, or a customer needs to be in there, a storage lease starts working against you.

Most business owners get to this question the same way. One unit becomes two, the garage at home fills up, and now you are driving to a gate at 6 a.m. to pull material for a job.

What can you actually do in a self-storage unit?

You can store inventory, tools, and equipment, and almost every facility lease stops you well short of operating from the unit. That means no employees clocking in there, no customers coming by, no running machinery or compressors, no vehicle work, and no plumbing of any kind. Facilities enforce it because their insurance and their certificate of occupancy are written for storage, and breaking those terms can cost you the unit and your coverage on what is inside it.

Is self-storage cheaper than flex space per square foot?

Not always, and the two pricing formats hide the comparison because storage is quoted per unit per month while flex is quoted per square foot per year. Convert the storage number yourself before you decide:

  • The formula: monthly rent × 12 ÷ unit square feet = annual rate per square foot.
  • Unit sizes: a 10x10 is 100 SF, a 10x20 is 200 SF, a 10x30 is 300 SF.
  • Worked line: $300 a month for a 10x20 is $3,600 a year over 200 SF, or $18.00 per square foot per year.
  • The flex side: quote your base rate plus the NNN estimate, since that sum is the real number. Our worked example in how much it costs to lease flex space in DFW runs $16.00 base plus about $4.50 NNN.

Run both through the same arithmetic and small storage units often land in the same per-foot range as a flex bay that comes with power, water, and a door. The absolute dollars are the honest difference: 200 square feet at $300 is a smaller check than 1,500 square feet at any rate, and you are buying far less of everything.

When should you move from storage to a flex unit?

Move when you are paying for a second unit, or when anyone needs to work in the space, whichever comes first. Three 10x20 units come to 600 square feet, which is under half of a 1,500 SF bay, and you are still driving between gates with no restroom and no place to plug in a saw. The other triggers are people: your first hire, a customer who wants to see the product, an inspector who needs an address, or a job that means assembly, repair, or vehicle work. Any one of those makes a storage lease the wrong document. If you are already past that line, what size flex space your business needs walks the 1,500, 2,000, and 3,000 SF options.

Then ask one question about the space itself. Does anyone need to work in it? If the answer is yes, a storage lease will not let you, at any price.

FAQ

Can you run a business out of a self-storage unit?

You can store inventory, tools, and equipment in a self-storage unit, and most facility leases stop short of letting you operate from it, which rules out staffing it, meeting customers there, running machinery, and working on vehicles. Storing is allowed at almost every facility, and working is usually not.

Is self-storage cheaper than flex space per square foot?

Not always, because the two are quoted in different units and the formats hide the comparison. Multiply the storage rent by 12, divide by the unit's square footage, and compare that number to a flex quote of base rent plus the NNN estimate.

How many storage units equal one flex bay?

Three 10x20 units come to 600 square feet, which is under half of a 1,500 SF flex bay, and none of that 600 feet has a dedicated electrical panel, a restroom, or a door a truck can back into. Most tenants who reach a second or third unit move once and stop paying for both.
